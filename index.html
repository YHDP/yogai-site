How Yogaí is made

The lessons are written with AI and checked by a person. A qualified yoga teacher reviews every lesson line by line before it ships: pose order, breath cues, alignment, how long you hold and where the silences fall. Nothing goes out that a teacher has not signed off.

The voice is synthesised. No human teacher recorded these sessions. We build the voice ourselves instead of licensing one, and that is what lets you choose. In a studio class you get whatever the teacher put on that morning. Here the same lesson exists as more than one finished recording, and you pick which one you get before you press play.

A recording cannot see you. It will not correct your alignment, notice that today is not the day for a deep twist, or adapt to an injury. Where a pose carries risk we say so out loud, for everyone. The rest is on you, and on a real teacher if you have one.

Opening up how we build it

Being written. Not ready to show.

Yogaí is put together from parts that could be useful to other people: a way of writing a class down so it can be produced consistently, a review loop between a teacher and the build, and the sound work underneath. None of that is secret, and most of it is worth more shared than kept.

We are writing that down properly, so a teacher could take it and produce her own classes rather than only listen to ours.
